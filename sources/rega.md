## Role and Mandate

The Real Estate General Authority (REGA) is the primary regulatory body for the real estate sector in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Established by Royal Decree No. M/838 dated 1441 AH, REGA operates under the supervision of the Council of Ministers and is responsible for organizing, regulating, and developing the real estate market across all its segments — residential, commercial, industrial, and agricultural.

REGA's core mandate includes licensing real estate brokers and brokerage offices, regulating real estate developers and off-plan sales, supervising rental market practices, and overseeing the platforms that digitize real estate transactions. It works in coordination with the Ministry of Municipalities and Housing, the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA), the Capital Market Authority (CMA), and the Ministry of Justice to ensure a unified, transparent, and well-governed real estate ecosystem aligned with Saudi Vision 2030.

As part of Vision 2030's housing program objectives, REGA plays a central role in raising the homeownership rate for Saudi citizens to 70%, attracting foreign real estate investment, and professionalizing the brokerage and development industries. REGA publishes quarterly and annual real estate market reports, maintains public registries of licensed brokers and developers, and issues regulatory guidelines and circulars that govern day-to-day real estate activity in the Kingdom.

## Key Regulations Issued

| Regulation (Arabic) | Regulation (English) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| نظام التطوير العقاري 1443هـ | Real Estate Development Law 1443H | Governs off-plan sales, developer licensing, escrow accounts, project registration on wafi |
| نظام الوساطة العقارية | Real Estate Brokerage Law | Governs broker licensing, commission limits, advertising standards, professional conduct |
| نظام الإيجار | Rental Law (Lease Regulation) | Governs residential and commercial lease agreements, tenant/landlord rights, ejar registration requirements |
| لوائح التسجيل العقاري (REDS) | Real Estate Documentation System Regulations | Governs the digital registration of property deeds and ownership transfers |

## Data Available from REGA

REGA publishes and makes accessible the following categories of data:

- **Licensed Brokers List:** Searchable via the nafitha platform. Includes broker name, license number, license expiry date, and affiliated office.
- **Licensed Developers List:** Searchable via the wafi platform. Includes developer name, wafi license number, and registered projects.
- **Registered Off-Plan Projects:** Available on wafi. Includes project name, location, developer, escrow bank, and completion status.
- **Market Reports:** Quarterly and annual real estate market reports available at rega.gov.sa/en/reports. Includes transaction volumes, price indices by region, and sector breakdowns.
- **Regulatory Circulars and Decisions:** Published on the REGA website under the regulations section.

## How to Use REGA Data in This Repository

When referencing REGA data or regulations in any file within this repository:

1. **Cite rega.gov.sa** as the primary source URL.
2. **Include the publication date** of the specific regulation or report you are referencing. REGA regulations are versioned by Hijri year — always note both the Hijri (هـ) and Gregorian year.
3. **Verify current status** before including any specific requirement, fee, or threshold. REGA periodically issues amendments and new circulars that supersede earlier guidance.
4. **Use the nafitha platform** to verify any specific broker license status — do not rely on static lists.
5. **Use the wafi platform** to verify any specific developer or project registration.
6. **Do not paraphrase regulatory text** without citing the original Arabic text; translation approximations must be clearly labeled as unofficial translations.

## Relevant API and Open Data Endpoints

REGA does not currently publish a fully documented public API. The following endpoints are known to provide publicly accessible data and are used in this repository for reference:

- Broker license verification: https://nafitha.rega.gov.sa (web-based search)
- Wafi project lookup: https://wafi.rega.gov.sa (web-based search)
- Market reports download: https://rega.gov.sa/en/reports

If REGA publishes an official open data API in the future, this section will be updated. Do not reverse-engineer or scrape REGA platforms in ways that violate their terms of service.
