# ejar — منصة إيجار

## Official Names

- **Arabic:** منصة إيجار
- **English:** ejar Platform

## Website

https://ejar.sa

## Owner and Operator

**REGA — الهيئة العامة للعقار (Real Estate General Authority)**
ejar is operated under the direct mandate of REGA and is the official government platform for rental contract registration in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

---

## Purpose

ejar was established to digitize the rental market in Saudi Arabia, bringing transparency, legal enforceability, and consumer protection to residential and commercial lease agreements. Prior to ejar, the Saudi rental market operated largely on informal written or verbal agreements with limited legal recourse for tenants or landlords in disputes.

The platform makes all residential and commercial lease agreements legally binding when registered, integrates with national identity and banking systems to verify parties and payment terms, and provides a centralized channel for maintenance requests and dispute escalation. ejar is a cornerstone of REGA's broader strategy to formalize the rental market as part of Vision 2030's housing and real estate objectives.

---

## How ejar Contracts Work

Registration on ejar requires both the tenant and the landlord (or their authorized representatives) to participate. The process works as follows:

1. **Identity Verification:** Both parties must provide their Saudi national ID (هوية وطنية) or Iqama (إقامة) number. The platform links to the National Information Center (NIC) to verify identity.

2. **Property Information:** The property must be identified by its REDS deed number or address. This links the contract to the official property registry.

3. **Contract Terms Entry:** Parties enter the rental amount in Saudi Riyals (SAR), the lease duration (start and end dates), payment frequency (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual), and maintenance responsibilities.

4. **IBAN Registration:** Both the landlord's IBAN and, where applicable, the tenant's IBAN must be registered. This enables electronic rent payments and supports the Saudi Central Bank's (SAMA) financial inclusion objectives.

5. **Electronic Signing:** Both parties electronically sign the contract via the platform using their verified national identities. No physical signature is required.

6. **Registration and Reference Number:** Upon signing, ejar generates a unique contract reference number. This number is the legal identifier of the contract and should be retained by both parties.

7. **Regulatory Reporting:** The registered contract is automatically reported to REGA, enabling market data collection on rental prices, occupancy rates, and geographic distribution.

## Key Features

- **Electronic Contract Registration:** Fully digital lease agreement creation, signing, and storage with legal enforceability.
- **Maintenance Requests:** Tenants can log maintenance requests through the platform; landlords can track and respond. The platform creates a documented record useful in dispute resolution.
- **Dispute Resolution:** ejar provides an escalation mechanism for landlord-tenant disputes. Cases can be submitted through the platform and referred to the relevant judicial or regulatory authority.
- **Automatic Renewal Management:** The platform supports contract renewal notifications and handles automatic renewal clauses in accordance with نظام الإيجار.
- **Sub-leasing Management:** Rules for sub-leasing (إيجار من الباطن) are tracked and regulated through the platform.
- **Financial Transaction Records:** Payment history is logged within the platform, supporting proof-of-payment in disputes.

---

## Legal Status of ejar Contracts

Contracts registered on ejar have full legal standing in Saudi Arabia. Under نظام الإيجار and REGA regulatory decisions:

- A registered ejar contract is legally enforceable without requiring notarization.
- Eviction proceedings and rental dispute resolution by Saudi courts and REGA's specialized rental dispute committees rely on the ejar-registered contract as the primary document.
- Unregistered contracts, while not automatically void, carry significantly reduced legal standing and make dispute resolution more difficult for both parties.
- Landlords who fail to register contracts on ejar may be subject to regulatory penalties under REGA's enforcement framework.
