# Example: Foreign Investor Query — Property Ownership Rights in Saudi Arabia

## Question

> **Investor's Question:**
> "I'm an American citizen working in Riyadh on an Iqama (residency permit). I've been living here for 3 years and want to buy an apartment instead of renting. My company also wants to acquire an office building for its Saudi operations. Can I personally buy an apartment? Can my company buy commercial property? Are there areas I should avoid? Where do I start?"

---

## Model Response

### Executive Summary

As an Iqama-holding resident, you have two distinct paths — one for personal residential ownership and one for corporate commercial acquisition. Both are legally possible with the right structure, but each has specific requirements and restrictions you must know upfront.

---

### 1. Personal Apartment Purchase (as an Individual)

**Can you do it?** Yes, with conditions.

Saudi regulations permit foreign nationals holding a valid Iqama to purchase **one residential property** for personal use, subject to:

- Your Iqama must be valid and in good standing
- The property must be for **your personal residence**, not for rental income or investment
- The property cannot be located in **Mecca or Medina** (prohibited for non-Muslims regardless of residency status)
- You may not own more than one residential property in this capacity
- The purchase must go through standard REGA registration (REDS system) and notarization

**Step-by-step process:**

| Step | Action | Authority | Where |
|------|--------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Confirm your Iqama is valid and shows Riyadh as residence | Ministry of Interior | Absher (absher.com.sa) |
| 2 | Find your property and sign a sale agreement | — | With seller and licensed broker |
| 3 | Conduct title deed verification | REGA | REDS (rega.gov.sa) |
| 4 | Pay Real Estate Transaction Tax (5% of price) | ZATCA | zatca.gov.sa |
| 5 | Notarize the sale contract | Ministry of Justice | كاتب العدل |
| 6 | Register ownership transfer in REDS | REGA | rega.gov.sa |

**Estimated timeline:** 4–8 weeks from signing to title deed issuance, depending on property status.

---

### 2. Corporate Office Acquisition (Company Buying Commercial Property)

**Can your company do it?** Yes, with a MISA investment license.

A foreign company (or a Saudi-registered subsidiary) can acquire commercial real estate in Saudi Arabia for its own operational use or as an investment asset, provided:

- The company holds a valid **MISA investment license** (رخصة استثمار)
- The property use aligns with the company's licensed business activity
- The company has valid commercial registration (سجل تجاري) in Saudi Arabia

**Recommended structure:** Establish a Saudi LLC (شركة ذات مسؤولية محدودة) as a wholly-owned subsidiary of your company. The Saudi entity can then own real estate directly, which provides the cleanest legal structure and full property rights.

**MISA licensing process:**

1. Apply at [misa.gov.sa](https://misa.gov.sa) for an investment license in your industry sector
2. Register the Saudi LLC with the Ministry of Commerce
3. Open a Saudi corporate bank account
4. Acquire property through the Saudi entity (REGA REDS registration applies)

**Timeline:** MISA license typically takes 2–4 weeks. Company formation adds 2–4 additional weeks.

---

### 3. Alternative: REITs (Lowest Barrier to Entry)

If you want real estate exposure without the legal complexity of direct purchase, Saudi **REITs (صناديق الاستثمار العقاري المتداولة)** listed on Tadawul (Saudi Exchange) are available to all investors, including foreign nationals and non-residents, with no MISA license required.

- Open a brokerage account with a Tadawul-licensed broker
- Purchase REIT units like stocks
- REITs must distribute at least 90% of net operating income annually
- Regulated by CMA; fully liquid

---

### 4. Restricted and Special Zones

| Area | Restriction |
|------|-------------|
| Mecca (مكة المكرمة) | Prohibited for non-Muslims. No exceptions. |
| Medina (المدينة المنورة) | Prohibited for non-Muslims. No exceptions. |
| NEOM | Special economic zone; separate regulations. Check with NEOM Company and MISA. |
| Red Sea Project / Amaala | Special tourism zone; check current foreign investment terms with MISA. |
| Military / security zones | Prohibited. Properties flagged in REGA system. |

---

### 5. Key Documents You Will Need

**For personal purchase:**
- Valid passport + Saudi Iqama
- Proof of income / source of funds (for AML compliance)
- Saudi bank account for property payments and IBAN for REGA registration

**For corporate purchase:**
- MISA investment license
- Saudi company commercial registration
- Board resolution authorizing the purchase
- Authorized signatory documentation

---

### 6. Risks and Considerations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Iqama expiry before title deed issuance | Renew Iqama before starting the process; keep at least 12 months validity |
| Off-plan purchase risk | Always verify project on wafi.rega.gov.sa before paying |
| Tax obligations | 5% Real Estate Transaction Tax is due; consult ZATCA for VAT implications on commercial property |
| Currency risk | All Saudi real estate transactions are in SAR; SAR is pegged to USD (no FX risk for USD-based investors currently) |
| Regulatory changes | Foreign ownership rules can change; verify with MISA at transaction time |
